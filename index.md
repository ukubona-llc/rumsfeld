For a company at time *t*:

- **Scalar (0 Embodied)**: Final embodied value — optimal allocation decision, thermodynamic efficiency (profit per bit/compute/dollar).  
  *Metrics*: Net Income / Free Cash Flow, Earnings Per Share (EPS) growth, Return on Invested Capital (ROIC), Market capitalization, Enterprise Value, Capital efficiency (Revenue or FCF per $ capex), Dividend/buyback policy (if applicable).

**Invariant Across Layers**: Compression ratio = preserved signal / original dimensionality. Measured indirectly via margin resilience under scaling or capex.
